Privacy & Data Protection Policy

AQQAR Real Estate Application • Effective: 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how AQQAR collects, uses, protects, and handles your personal information when you use our mobile application and real estate platform. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency in all our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, editing your profile, or listing real estate properties. This includes your name, phone number, optional secondary business contact numbers, profile photo or agency logo, and real estate listing details (property specifications, prices, descriptions, location coordinates, and uploaded photographs). We may also collect diagnostic data such as device type and OS version solely to maintain system stability and optimize app performance.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is used strictly to provide and enhance our real estate services: displaying verified property listings, enabling direct communication between prospective buyers/renters and property owners/agents via phone or WhatsApp, verifying agency credentials, preventing fraudulent activity or spam, and providing customer support. We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers or marketing brokers.

3. Device Permissions & Transparency

Photos & Camera: Requested solely when you choose to capture or upload real estate property images or company logos.
Location: Used only with your permission to accurately position real estate listings on the interactive map or display nearby properties.

4. Data Security & Storage

All data is encrypted in transit and stored within secure, enterprise-grade cloud server infrastructure protected by industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) and strict access controls to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

You retain complete ownership of your personal data. You have the right to review, update, or permanently delete your account, listings, and personal information at any time. To request immediate and irreversible deletion of your account and all associated records, please email our support team at KBTECH2026K@GMAIL.COM. Deletion requests are processed completely within 48 hours.
